THOUGHTFUL BY DESIGN

Built to help you
make the call.

Cartonroom plans with rectangular item envelopes and works entirely in your browser. It checks dimensions, rotation, support for the padded envelopes and content weight. It does not calculate shipping rates, predict damage or guarantee the smallest possible arrangement.
